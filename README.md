An entry for [The Tired Dark Lord, A 100-Word Villain](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) on make.fun: a monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary.

## The monologue (99 words)

> Rise, my legions, rise. The skeletons filed a complaint this morning, something about the damp in the crypt and the lack of a pension plan. I raised them to conquer the valley, and they want ergonomic scythes. My chief wraith has taken up pottery. Pottery. The soul jars are labelled now, alphabetised, dusted weekly by a ghost who hums. I used to command armies that blotted out the sun. Now I mediate a rota dispute between the bone collectors and the grave diggers, and the grave diggers are winning. Strike me down, hero. The rota will still need signing.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (559 characters, 99 words — inside the gate of 1–600 characters and about 100 words). It also guards the gate itself: it refuses to write if the text drifts outside 90–110 words or 1–600 characters.
